About CVMS

Our Child Vaccination Management System is designed to streamline vaccination tracking for children. With role-based access for parents, hospitals, and admins, it ensures timely vaccinations and maintains complete records.

Built with modern web technologies, it provides a user-friendly interface for managing child profiles, scheduling appointments, and generating certificates.

Key Features

👥 User Management

Role-based authentication for Admin, Hospital, and Parent users.

👶 Child Profiles

Add and manage child records with medical history and vaccination schedules.

💉 Vaccination Tracking

Auto-generate schedules based on national guidelines and track completion status.

📅 Appointment Booking

Parents can book appointments, hospitals update status, admins approve.

Email and SMS reminders for upcoming and overdue vaccinations.
