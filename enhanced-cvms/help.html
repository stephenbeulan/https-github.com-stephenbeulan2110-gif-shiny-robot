Frequently Asked Questions

Managing Children

How do I add a new child?
+

To add a new child, go to the "Children" section and click "Add Child". Fill in their name, date of birth, gender, and blood type. Click "Save Child" to add them to your account.

Can I edit a child's information?
+

Yes, you can edit a child's information by clicking the "Edit" button on their card in the Children section. Make the necessary changes and save.

How do I delete a child's profile?
+

Click the "Delete" button on the child's card. You will be asked to confirm the deletion. Note that this action cannot be undone and will delete all associated records.

Vaccination Tracking

How do I update a vaccination record?
+

Go to the Vaccinations section, find the vaccination you want to update, and click on it. Update the status and date as needed. Click "Save" to update the record.

What do the different vaccination statuses mean?
+

Pending: Vaccination is scheduled but not yet completed. Completed: Vaccination has been administered. Overdue: The vaccination date has passed and it hasn't been completed.

Can I export my vaccination records?
+

Yes, you can export your vaccination records to CSV format by clicking the "Export to CSV" button on the Vaccinations page. This creates a downloadable file of all records.

What are the standard vaccinations for children?
+

Standard childhood vaccinations typically include: BCG, Polio, DPT (Diphtheria, Pertussis, Tetanus), Hepatitis B, Measles, Varicella (Chickenpox), and others depending on your country's immunization schedule. Consult with your healthcare provider for specific recommendations.

Appointments

How do I book an appointment?
+

Go to the Appointments section and click "Book Appointment". Select your child, vaccine type, date, time, and clinic. Fill in any additional notes and click "Book Appointment" to save.

Can I modify or cancel an appointment?
+

You can modify an appointment by clicking the "Edit" button. To cancel, click the "Delete" or "Cancel" option. Make sure to contact your clinic to inform them of any changes.

Will I get appointment reminders?
+

If you have notifications enabled in your Settings, you will receive email reminders before your scheduled appointments. You can customize notification preferences in the Settings section.

Account & Settings

How do I change my password?
+

Go to Settings > Account and scroll to "Change Password". Enter your current password, new password, and confirm it. Click "Change Password" to update.

How do I manage notification preferences?
+

Go to Settings > Notifications. You can toggle different notification types on/off, including email notifications, vaccination reminders, and appointment confirmations.

Is my personal data secure?
+

Yes, we take security seriously. Your data is encrypted and stored securely. You can view security settings in Settings > Privacy & Security to enable additional security features like two-factor authentication.

How do I delete my account?
+

Go to Settings > System and scroll to "Danger Zone". Click "Delete Account". You will be asked to confirm. This action will permanently delete all your data and cannot be undone.
