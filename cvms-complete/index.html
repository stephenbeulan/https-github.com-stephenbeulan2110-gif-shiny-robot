About CVMS

The Child Vaccination Management System (CVMS) is designed to streamline and modernize the vaccination process in India. Following the Universal Immunization Programme (UIP), our platform ensures that every child receives timely vaccinations while providing parents and healthcare providers with powerful tools to manage vaccination records.

Built with modern web technologies, CVMS offers a secure, user-friendly interface that works seamlessly across all devices. Our role-based system ensures that administrators, hospitals, and parents each have access to the tools they need while maintaining data privacy and security.
